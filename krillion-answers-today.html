How Krillion answers & scores work

Each Krillion day has seven prompts. You invent answers that the game accepts; rarer accepted answers earn more points. After the day unlocks, the public answer sheet shows every valid response with a tier and score. We mirror that sheet here so you can check a guess, study high-scoring Deep Cuts, or scan for One in a Krillion gems without guessing blindly.

  • 100 pts · One in a Krillion — the rarest accepted answers, often with a short quip.
  • ~85 pts · Deep Cut — obscure but valid; most of the long lists live here.
  • Rare / Schooler — mid-tier scores between deep cuts and everyday answers.
  • ~10 pts · Plankton — common answers almost everyone thinks of.

Popularity counts (when available) show how often an answer appears in community stats. A low-popularity answer can still be a mid score—and a high score is not guaranteed just because few people picked it. Use popularity as a hint, not a rule.

Krillion answers FAQ

What is Krillion?

Krillion is a daily word/trivia game with seven prompts. You submit creative answers; the game scores them by how rare they are among accepted responses.

When do today's Krillion answers unlock?

After the day's public answer sheet is released. Until then this page shows a waiting state and will not invent blank answer lists.

Do rare answers always score highest?

Not always. Score and tier come from the official sheet. Community popularity is a separate signal—useful for hunting obscure picks, but not a promise of 100 points.

Is this the official Krillion answers site?

No. We are an independent fan tool, not affiliated with Krillion. Play at krillion.io.
