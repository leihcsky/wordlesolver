How this Krillion archive works

Each Krillion day has seven prompts and thousands of accepted answers ranked by score. After a day unlocks, we save that answer sheet here so you can revisit high-scoring Deep Cuts, One in a Krillion gems, or common Plankton picks from earlier days.

  • One page, many dates — pick a day from the list; we load answers on this same archive page (no thin per-day URLs).
  • Scores & tiers stay intact — browse by prompt, tier, score, or popularity the same way as on today's page.
  • Launch-forward history — we only keep days saved after this tool launched. Older official sheets can't be backfilled.

Krillion archive FAQ

How do I find yesterday's Krillion answers?

Pick the date from Saved dates, then open each prompt or search for the answer you typed. Scores and tiers match what the game accepted that day.

Why isn't an older day listed?

We only keep days we've saved since this tool launched. If you don't see a date, we never stored that day's answers and can't recreate them.

How do I find rare or high-scoring answers from a past day?

Select the day, filter by tier (like One in a Krillion or Deep Cut), sort by score, or sort by popularity from low to high to surface uncommon picks.

Is this the official Krillion site?

No—just an independent fan tool. Play the real game at krillion.io.
